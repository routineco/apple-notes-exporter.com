The migration path

How to move from Apple Notes to Google Keep

Google Keep does not offer a direct import from Apple Notes. The best approach is to export your Apple Notes to a portable format first, then transfer them into Google Keep manually or via Google Drive.

1

Export your Apple Notes

Use Apple Notes Exporter to convert your notes to HTML or plain text. These formats are easy to copy and paste into Google Keep.

2

Open the exported files

Open the HTML files in any browser. The content is clean, readable, and easy to select and copy.

3

Create notes in Google Keep

For each exported note, create a new note in Google Keep and paste the content. For long notes, consider Google Docs instead for richer formatting support.

4

Or store in Google Drive

For a bulk migration, upload your exported HTML or Markdown files directly to Google Drive. They are fully searchable and accessible on any device.

Which format to choose?

HTML or Markdown for Google Keep?

🌐

HTML — best for Google Keep

HTML files open instantly in any browser. Easy to copy-paste content directly into Google Keep notes. Preserves text structure visually.

📄

PDF — best for archiving

If you want a permanent, read-only archive of your Apple Notes alongside Google Keep, PDF is the cleanest option.

📝

Markdown — best for Google Docs

Markdown files can be imported into Google Docs with formatting plugins, making them ideal for longer, more structured notes.
