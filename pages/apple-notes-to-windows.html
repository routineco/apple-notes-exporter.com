The options

Three ways to access Apple Notes on Windows

Depending on your use case — switching entirely, working across both systems, or just archiving — a different export format will suit you best.

📄

PDF — open anywhere

PDF files open natively in Microsoft Edge, Adobe Reader, or any PDF viewer on Windows. No setup, no conversion needed.

🌐

HTML — in your browser

HTML files open in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on Windows. Your notes are readable and searchable immediately.

📝

Markdown — in Obsidian or Notion

If you're moving to a note app that runs on Windows, Markdown export gives you editable files compatible with Obsidian, Notion, Typora and more.

Step by step

How to transfer Apple Notes to a Windows PC

1

Export on your Mac

Use Apple Notes Exporter on your Mac to export your notes to PDF or HTML. Select all folders for a complete export.

2

Transfer to your Windows PC

Copy the exported files to a USB drive, upload them to OneDrive or Google Drive, or share them via a network folder. They'll sync automatically on your PC.

3

Open on Windows

PDF files open in Microsoft Edge out of the box. HTML files open in any browser. Markdown files open in Obsidian, VS Code, or Notepad++.
